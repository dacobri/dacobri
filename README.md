I sit between the business and the build. I scope what a problem is actually worth solving, then ship the tool that solves it — AI assistants, agentic systems, and decision dashboards that people can use, not slideware. My work spans applied machine learning, LLM/agent products, and analytics that turn data into decisions.

What I bring:

- **Frame the problem** — translate a vague business need into something measurable and worth building.
- **Build the thing** — prototype fast with LLMs, multi-agent systems, and ML, end to end.
- **Make it a decision** — turn models and data into dashboards and recommendations a non-technical stakeholder can act on.
